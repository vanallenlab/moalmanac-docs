We have updated the license and policies that govern the Molecular Oncology Almanac. Our database is now available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0), and our [terms of use](../../about/terms-of-use.md), [disclaimer](../../about/disclaimer.md), and [privacy policy](../../about/privacy-policy.md) have been rewritten and are effective as of October 24, 2026.

<!-- more -->

## Licensing

Previously, the database was released under the Open Database License (ODbL), which required that derived databases be shared under the same license. We have moved to [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which permits any use, including commercial use, so long as appropriate credit is given. We hope this makes it easier to build on and integrate our work. Our software, including the [API](https://api.moalmanac.org), [browsers](https://moalmanac.org), and [interpretation algorithm](https://github.com/vanallenlab/moalmanac), remains licensed under [GPL v2](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.en.html).

## Terms of use and disclaimer

The terms of use now describe the licenses and general conditions of use, and no longer act as the license themselves. Our previous restrictions prohibiting clinical use and use as the basis of a commercial product have been removed. In their place, a new [disclaimer](../../about/disclaimer.md) states that the database and software have not been validated for diagnostic or clinical use and come with no warranty, and that users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, institutional policies, and ethical requirements, including those for research with human subjects.

## Privacy policy

The privacy policy now covers our API and browsers. We continue to use [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/), which sets no cookies and stores no personal data, not even IP addresses. We have removed language about collecting email addresses for database contributions, as well as sections on data transfers and disclosures, as the [Google Chrome extension](https://github.com/vanallenlab/moalmanac-extension) has been deprecated.

If you have any questions or concerns, please [contact us](../../about/contact.md).

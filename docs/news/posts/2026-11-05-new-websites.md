[https://moalmanac.org](https://moalmanac.org) now points to our new browser and database schema, which follows the [GA4GH Genomic Knowledge Model](https://ga4gh.github.io/gkm-starter-kit/latest/) that we [announced as a work in progress](2025-03-06-gks-in-development.md) last year. We have also launched [docs.moalmanac.org](https://docs.moalmanac.org) as a centralized documentation website for all of our services.

## The new browser and schema

The new browser and database are built on the GA4GH Genomic Knowledge Model. Over the coming weeks, we will be migrating non-approval genomic knowledge statements from the old schema to the new one.

## The old website and database

The previous version of the browser and database remains available at [https://old.moalmanac.org](https://old.moalmanac.org) and [on GitHub](https://github.com/vanallenlab/moalmanac-db/blob/main/molecular-oncology-almanac.json). **Both will be deprecated on March 31st, 2027**.

## Documentation

[docs.moalmanac.org](https://docs.moalmanac.org) hosts documentation for the database, API, browser, and interpretation algorithm. The [terms of use](../../about/terms-of-use.md), [privacy policy](../../about/privacy-policy.md), [disclaimer](../../about/disclaimer.md), and [contact](../../about/contact.md) information can be found in the [About](../../about/index.md) section.

## News

We have migrated the content of news.moalmanac.org to this website, and news.moalmanac.org now redirects to [docs.moalmanac.org/news](https://docs.moalmanac.org/news). **The news repository and redirect will be deprecated on December 31st, 2026**.

If you have any questions or concerns, please [contact us](../../about/contact.md).
